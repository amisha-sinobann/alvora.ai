Data Sources & Methodology

The data presented in this comparison is aggregated and synthesized from a variety of reputable sources to provide a balanced overview. The "Effectiveness Score" is a proprietary metric calculated based on weighted pollutant removal efficiency against the total cost of ownership over a 10-year period.

  • Efficiency Ratings: Based on industry standards and lab tests for each technology type.
  • Cost Analysis: Includes initial purchase price plus estimated annual maintenance (filter replacements) and energy consumption costs.
  • Lifespan: Reflects the expected operational life of the core unit and key components.
  • Effectiveness Score: A higher score indicates better performance for the cost. The score is heavily influenced by the "Focus Pollutant" selected above.
